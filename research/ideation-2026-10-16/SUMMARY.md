# Ideation synthesis — 2026-10-16

- Nine personas have Codex responses (`codex-1.md` through `codex-9.md`); these are qualitative proposals, not measured demand or independently verified facts.
- Shopping persona: preserve instruction boundaries and distinguish merchant identity, payee binding and action-specific evidence from a site verdict.
- Coding persona: probe actual tools and versions, record last successful use, and keep private quota/credential state explicit and unknown when unavailable.
- Ops persona: join local failures with component/region incidents and maintenance; anonymous status access does not establish commercial redistribution permission.
- Research persona: review full primary sources, distinguish publication from effective dates, and track citation support, corrections and superseding evidence.
- Red-team persona: expose provenance and freshness while protecting instruction boundaries, tool descriptions, persistent memory and held-out expected answers; PSL/Unicode identity is advisory.
- Budget persona: verify actual allowances, billing boundaries, compatibility, licences and worst-case task cost; nominally free infrastructure can still block execution.
- Site-owner persona: report observed robots/access/redirect/challenge evidence, publisher entrypoints and bounded retry/remediation checks; label inferred causes.
- Payments and travel personas: use private spending authority, reconciliation/receipts and quote expiry; traveller-specific rules, inventory and global calendar coverage remain source-dependent unknowns.
