# Prioritised agent-web backlog

Merged from the previous backlog and [2026-10-16 research](research/2026-10-16-codex.md), ordered by avoiding unsafe actions before expanding context. Status describes shipped scope only; backlog clauses remain future work. Research-only sources retain their original licence caveats.

1. **User authority, identity and budgets** — prevent wrong-account actions, disclosure and unauthorized spending; keep session/tenant/expiry and tool/network capabilities local; [evidence](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/AI_Agent_Security_Cheat_Sheet.html); **backlog**.
2. **Signature, provenance, freshness and coverage** — prevent forged, expired or partial evidence becoming GO; signed context, original scan timestamps and explicit unknown/expired states shipped; [evidence](specs/verdict-rules.md); **live**.
3. **Untrusted evidence isolation and injection fixtures** — prevent page instructions controlling agents; capped structured findings shipped, extend fixtures to WAAA web/social-engineering attacks; [evidence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05509); **live**.
4. **Exact origins, registrable domains and verified route ownership** — prevent substituted credential/payment endpoints and shared-host false matches; same-origin routes and PSL-based phishing lookup shipped; [evidence](specs/threat-model.md); **live**.
5. **Independent phishing warnings** — add CERT Polska active-feed matches as TTL-bound STOP without treating absence as clean; [commercial permission](https://cert.pl/en/warning-list/); **live**.
6. **Access blocks, challenges and identification requirements** — avoid doomed tasks; existing probe/robots signals shipped, extend intent-specific checkout detection using the user-supplied Oct 2026 Amazon/Grok “unauthorized AI agent” report (unverified: X could not be retrieved); [reported evidence](https://x.com/IamSinovuyo/status/2110730714643832901); **live**.
7. **Observable task success, receipts and retry safety** — distinguish completion from correctness, prevent duplicate effects, capture missing fields and stale evidence; [evidence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20724); **backlog**.
8. **Rate limits, backoff and execution limits** — bound retries and stop on challenges/blocks; request caps and per-host spacing shipped, executor budget integration pending; [evidence](scanner/network.mjs); **live**.
9. **Private validated outcome ingestion and diverse aggregation** — capture failure stage without user content, resist fabricated success with quarantine/operator-weight bounds; [evidence](specs/threat-model.md); **backlog**.
10. **Read-only synthetic probes** — provide honest access/descriptor observations with sample sizes while withholding unsupported success/GO claims; [evidence](scanner/outcomes.mjs); **live**.
11. **Structured interfaces, versions and task recipes** — reduce fragile UI steps through verified APIs/MCP/feeds, authoritative discovery and repeatable paths; discovery ordering shipped, verified recipes remain pending; [evidence](specs/verdict-rules.md); **live**.
12. **Tool health, poisoning checks and live page state** — detect unsafe descriptions, unavailable integrations and stale controls before invocation; [evidence](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/AI_Agent_Security_Cheat_Sheet.html); **backlog**.
13. **Provider incidents and maintenance** — avoid mistaking outages for auth failures; retain tested status.mjs adapter for a future provider grant, keep current providers link-only; [evidence](DECISIONS.md); **skipped-licence**.
14. **Commercial reuse and operation-specific terms** — verify each source grant and terms classification before enabling reuse or recommending actions; current unverified sources stay link-only; [evidence](research/2026-10-16-codex.md#12-content-use-licence-site-terms-and-permitted-operations); **backlog**.

16. **Locale, units, places and entity identity** — prevent numeric ambiguity, wrong-city and same-name errors using reviewed CLDR/OSM/Wikidata sources; [evidence](research/2026-10-16-codex.md#17-locale-number-formats-currency-precision-and-units); **backlog**.
