How a verdict is decided

Evaluate STOP rules first. Any STOP rule means STOP. Otherwise, any CAUTION rule means CAUTION. If no rule fires, the snapshot is GO. Ruleset v0.1 is published in the repository.

STOP
Fresh phishing or malware listings; a brand lookalike younger than 90 days; high-severity prompt injection. Intent-specific rules cover high-confidence agent bans (except read/search), automated-account bans and future sanctions checks.
CAUTION
Blocked or challenged probes, robots restrictions, identification requirements, ambiguous terms, paid crawl responses, medium injection, young domains for sensitive intents, low or falling outcome success, missing purchase returns policy, stale data or no data.
GO
No rule fired. Independent sources and our own scans are needed to raise a verdict; outcomes alone cannot produce GO or clear a safety flag.

Confidence & freshness

High confidence requires all components within TTL, at least 20 outcomes and no ambiguous terms. Medium covers smaller samples or a component between one and two TTLs old. Stale data, missing data or ambiguous terms lowers confidence. ToS classification is deferred in static mode: not yet classified.

Outcome rates are hidden below n = 5. Verdict changes from outcomes require at least three independent verified operators and probe confirmation; one operator contributes at most 30% weight.

Routes & trust

Routes are ordered by observed working, discovered, announced only, unknown, then broken. Within a status: API, MCP, UCP, ACP, PAP, A2A, NLWeb, WebMCP, structured feed, markdown, browser. Success rates only break ties with n ≥ 5.

No payment or credential destinations. Routes must come from our own scanner on the same registrable domain. Evidence spans are capped at 200 characters and marked untrusted. Safety flags require human review to clear.

Sources & neutrality

Published reasons identify their source and check date. Own scans observe HTTP responses, robots rules and deterministic injection patterns. The source register records candidate feeds and licence status; inclusion there does not mean a feed is active or licensed for reuse.

Cloudflare Radar and xpay are external comparisons, linked rather than copied. No paid ranking, paid verdict changes or paid verified badges. The leaderboard is alphabetical, not a merchant quality ranking.
